About Us

Made with care. Served with love.

At Edible Treats, we believe good food is more than just something to eat — it is part of how we connect, celebrate and create memories.

From the crispy crunch of samosa and spring rolls to soft puff-puff, tasty buns and well-seasoned peppered meat, these are the kinds of treats many of us grew up enjoying and continue to love.

Our love for food goes beyond snacks. We also prepare home-style Nigerian meals and catering for everyday enjoyment, family gatherings, celebrations and special occasions.

Edible Treats brings together the familiar flavours we love with the care and warmth of food made for people.
